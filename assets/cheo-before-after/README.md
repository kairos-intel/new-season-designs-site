# Cheo — Remodel Photos (Before / After), Classified

52 client photos of real renovation jobs, sorted into 17 job folders. Files are named
`before-N.jpg`, `during-N.jpg`, `after-N.jpg` (plus room labels in the big condo job).
All shots are iPhone-quality portrait/landscape mixes, roughly 375–500px wide previews,
full-res originals. Coastal South Florida homes (visible in the pool/exterior shots).

**Best true before→after pairs for side-by-side sliders are marked ⭐.**

## Notes for the coding agent

- ⭐ folders have genuine same-room before/after pairs — use these for
  before/after slider components. Folders 01, 03, 08, 12, 15 are clean 1-to-1 pairs.
- 14-garage-to-studio supports a 3-step (before/during/after) sequence.
- "After only" folders (04, 05, 09, 10, 16, 17) are portfolio/gallery material.
- Photos are unedited phone shots: mixed portrait/landscape, some clutter/people
  reflections (a person is faintly reflected in `08/before-1.jpg` mirror and standing at
  the edge of `16/after-2.jpg`). Crop or pick accordingly.
- Filenames were originally UUIDs with no EXIF; grouping/classification was done
  visually. The pairings in 03 and 07 are high-confidence but inferred from matching
  layouts/finishes rather than identical camera angles.
- Full-size originals also live at
  `~/new season designs /cheo remodel photos before and after : client photos `
  (both folder names contain trailing spaces).
