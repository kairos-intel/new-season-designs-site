THE NEW SEASON STORY

Jose “Cheo” Rodriguez leads every project with responsibility, communication, and care.

Cheo, Co-Founder and Lead Designer at New Season Designs, LLC, has built the company around a simple promise: homeowners should not have to carry the stress of chasing contractors, suppliers, and unanswered questions. NSD gives each client one real person who understands the vision, sets clear expectations, and keeps the work moving in a streamlined way.

The company began in 2020, when Cheo and his wife Ginny started taking on handyman work after the pandemic changed their previous venture. That beginning has grown into a full remodeling company handling kitchens, bathrooms, concrete, plumbing, electrical, painting, and complete interior and exterior renovations with a trusted group of specialists.

THE ACCOUNTABILITY STANDARD

When something changes, you should hear it from us first.

Remodels can reveal conditions no one could see at the start. The difference is how those moments are handled. We explain what changed, outline the impact, and ask for your approval before added work proceeds. NSD does not pass responsibility down the chain; Cheo remains responsible for the communication and follow-through.
